## Deliberately not asked

- **Anything about patients.** No counts, no conditions, no case studies, no testimonials.
  Asking the question creates the data problem.
- **Their regulatory position.** Not Webgrove's call to assess or advise on — see
  [04-compliance-constraints.md](04-compliance-constraints.md).

## How to ask

Over WhatsApp, in one short message, in a batch they can answer in a single reply — not a
call. Clinic days are busy and daytime hours are theirs, not Mahabeer's. B1–B5 plus C5 and C6
are one message; the rest can wait for the kickoff checklist in
[client-facing/kickoff-inputs.md](client-facing/kickoff-inputs.md).
