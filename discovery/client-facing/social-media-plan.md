# Social media plan — Daiviksatva

Prepared for Pawan · Date: 20 October 2026 · A three-month plan

## The idea

You have something most clinics advertising on Instagram do not: practitioners who can explain
a treatment properly on camera. That is the whole plan. I am not going to post stock images and
motivational quotes — I am going to turn your team's knowledge into a steady stream of short,
useful videos and posts, publish them on a fixed schedule across four platforms, and show you
what it produced each month.

Three months is enough to find out whether it works. It is not enough to build a large
following, and I would rather say that now than oversell it.

This is separate from the website and runs on its own. Nothing here is committed until you
confirm.

## Content pillars

Everything published falls into one of five buckets, so the feed stays varied without anyone
having to invent ideas each week:

1. **Treatment explainers** — a practitioner explains one treatment: what it involves, what a
   session feels like, who it suits
2. **Seasonal and daily Ayurveda** — practical guidance tied to the season and the weather
3. **Diet and yoga** — short, usable advice
4. **Inside the clinic** — the premises, the team, your in-house medicine preparation
5. **Questions you are actually asked** — the ones patients ask at reception, answered properly

**Two things that will not be published, on any platform:** patient testimonials or patient
videos, and any claim that a treatment cures a named disease. The same rules that apply to the
website apply here, and social media is watched more closely, not less. The reasoning is in the
website plan. Your practitioners' own authority is stronger content anyway.

## Month 1

### Days 1–15 — setup and automation

- Create or clean up all four accounts: profile pictures, bios, about sections, contact buttons,
  WhatsApp button, highlights, pinned posts
- Link everything to your Google Business Profile so the clinic is consistent everywhere
- Agree the first month's planner with you
- Collect the first batch of raw footage — one filming session covers several weeks of content
- **Set up automated publishing** on the server you provide, so posts go out on schedule
  without anyone remembering to do it

**An honest note on the automation.** I run this already for Webgrove's own accounts, so the
system exists and works. Pointing it at _your_ accounts has two gates that are outside my
control:

- **Instagram and Facebook:** Meta requires an application review before an app can publish to
  accounts it does not own. That review typically takes two to four weeks and the timing is
  Meta's, not mine.
- **LinkedIn:** company page posting is restricted to LinkedIn's approved partners, and that
  approval is genuinely difficult to obtain. I will post to LinkedIn by hand, or we use a paid
  third-party scheduler if you would rather — that is a small extra subscription and I would ask
  you before buying anything.
- **YouTube** automates without any gate.

So: **publishing starts on day 16 either way.** It simply starts manually on some platforms and
switches over to automation as each approval lands. Nothing in your schedule slips because of
it — I just wanted you to hear this from me now rather than wonder later.

## What I am not promising

Worth being straight about this, because plenty of agencies are not:

- **No follower count guarantees.** Anyone who promises you numbers is buying fake ones
- **No guaranteed enquiries.** Three months tells us whether this channel works for your clinic
- **I am one person.** You deal with me directly, and I would rather commit to a schedule I can
  actually hold than to a volume I cannot

One thing I will say plainly: both enquiries Webgrove itself has received so far came through
**Google search**, with no social posting at all. That is why the website plan puts your Google
Business Profile first, and it is free. Social media is worth doing — but if you only have
budget for one thing right now, do the website and the Google listing first.
